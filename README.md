# Green Valley Medical Center – Healthcare Data Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyses patient data from Green Valley Medical Center to identify patterns in patient recovery, treatment costs, hospital stay duration, demographics and clinical performance.

The analysis covers 400 patient records and uses an interactive dashboard to present key healthcare performance indicators and support data-driven decision-making.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Data Cleaning and Preparation
- Calculated Fields
- Data Visualisation
- Dashboard Design
- Healthcare Data Analysis

## Key Performance Indicators

- Total Patients: 400
- Average Recovery Score: 75.34/100
- Average Treatment Cost: £5,052
- Average Hospital Stay: 5.35 days
- Average Patient Age: approximately 45 years

## Analysis Areas

The project explored:

- Patient recovery by department
- Treatment effectiveness
- Treatment costs
- Length of hospital stay
- Patient demographics
- Gender patterns
- Doctor performance
- Department performance

## Key Insights

### Patient Recovery

Dermatology and General Medicine recorded the highest average recovery
scores at 76.61 and 76.57 respectively.

Cardiology recorded the lowest average recovery score at 72.92 while
having one of the higher average treatment costs at £5,255.

This suggests an opportunity to review patient pathways, case complexity
and post-treatment follow-up within Cardiology.

### Treatment Effectiveness and Cost

Surgery achieved the highest average recovery score of 76.84.

However, it was also the most expensive and longest-stay treatment type,
with:

- Average treatment cost: £12,619
- Average hospital stay: 10.07 days

Observation had the lowest average cost (£1,080) and shortest stay
(2.09 days), but also had a lower recovery score of 72.86.

### Demographic Patterns

Young Adults aged 20–35 recorded the highest average recovery score
of 76.20.

Teen patients aged 13–19 recorded the lowest average recovery score
of 73.93.

Differences between male and female patients were relatively small.

### Doctor Performance

Dr. Johnson achieved the highest average recovery score at 78.51
and demonstrated the strongest excellent recovery rate.

Dr. Smith recorded the lowest average recovery score at 69.78.

Further investigation could determine whether differences are associated
with case mix, treatment allocation, documentation quality or
follow-up processes.

## Recommendations

1. Prioritise a Cardiology improvement review focusing on the
   diagnosis-to-treatment workflow, treatment mix and discharge follow-up.

2. Compare Surgery outcomes against costs to determine where higher
   recovery outcomes justify greater resource use and where savings
   may be possible.

3. Review Observation protocols because the treatment type is
   cost-effective but produces weaker recovery results.

4. Share best practices from Dermatology, General Medicine and
   high-performing doctors across departments.
